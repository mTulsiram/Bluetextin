Global Privacy Policy & Data Protection Statement

Effective Date: 1 January 2026 | Last Comprehensive Regulatory Review: 2026.1

Core Commitment: BlueTEXT.in operates with a strict 100% Client-Side Execution & Zero-Tracker Policy. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or transfer your personal data, code, files, videos, or documents to any third-party servers. All computation executes locally inside your web browser.

1. Data Minimization & Architecture (EU GDPR Art. 5, India DPDP Act 2023)

In accordance with data minimization principles, BlueTEXT.in collects zero telemetry or behavioral tracking identifiers. Tool inputs (video timelines, JSON schemas, SQL queries, regex patterns, text files) reside solely in your local browser memory (RAM/IndexedDB/localStorage) and are never transmitted over the network.

2. Local Storage & Cookie Classification (ePrivacy Directive & GDPR Art. 6)

We do not use tracking or third-party advertising cookies. We utilize strictly necessary local browser storage for:

  • bt_theme_v1: Preserves your selected dark/light interface preference.
  • bt_consent_v1: Stores your granular consent preferences.
  • bt_auth_user_v1: Stores optional, user-initiated local sign-in state.

3. United States Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA & State Regulations)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

  • No Sale or Sharing: BlueTEXT.in does not sell or share personal information as defined by California law.
  • Right to Know & Delete: As we store no server-side personal profiles, you maintain total data sovereignty directly through your browser settings by clearing your local storage or clicking the "Clear Cache" button.

4. European Union & UK GDPR Rights (Articles 13–22)

Data subjects in the EU and UK possess the following statutory rights:

  • Right of Access & Portability: All project data in our tools can be exported directly to local files (e.g. JSON, MP4, WebM, CSV).
  • Right to Erasure: One-click local data eradication is accessible across all tools via the "Clear Cache" button in the global navigation bar.
  • Data Protection Officer Inquiries: Contact our designated privacy officer at admin@bluetext.in.

5. India Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 Compliance

As a digital platform rooted in Hyderabad, India, BlueTEXT.in adheres strictly to the DPDP Act 2023 and Information Technology Rules:

  • Data Fiduciary: BlueTEXT.in (Admin: admin@bluetext.in).
  • Grievance Officer: Grievances can be submitted directly to admin@bluetext.in with a guaranteed 48-hour response SLA.

6. China Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL)

BlueTEXT.in performs no cross-border transmission or automated profiling of personal information. All browser applications operate on a standalone, decentralized client-side basis.

7. Security & Content Security Policy (CSP)

Our platform enforces strict HTTPS/HSTS and Content Security Policies prohibiting unauthorized third-party script injection or unauthorized socket connections.

8. Contact Information

For any privacy inquiries, audits, or verification requests, contact us at admin@bluetext.in or via our Contact Form.
