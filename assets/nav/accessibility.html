Accessibility Conformance Statement & VPAT Overview

Standard Conformance: WCAG 2.1 & 2.2 Level AA | US Section 508 | EN 301 549 (EAA)

Accessibility Guarantee: BlueTEXT.in is engineered to ensure universal accessibility for users with disabilities, assistive technology operators, and neurodivergent individuals worldwide.

1. Technical Conformance Matrix

WCAG Criterion Requirement Status
1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum) 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large/UI components ✅ Conforms (AAA/AA)
2.1.1 Keyboard Navigable All interactive elements reachable without mouse ✅ Conforms
2.4.1 Bypass Blocks Skip-to-main-content landmark link provided ✅ Conforms
2.4.7 Focus Visible High-contrast 2px focus ring on interactive items ✅ Conforms
2.2.2 Pause, Stop, Hide Zero unprompted blinking or auto-playing media ✅ Conforms
2.3.3 Animation from Interactions prefers-reduced-motion & high contrast queries ✅ Conforms

2. Assistive Technology Compatibility

Our single-file tools and website layouts are verified across screen readers (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver, TalkBack) and switch devices.

3. Formal Feedback & Remediation SLA

If you encounter any barrier or have feedback on assistive usability, please contact our Accessibility Lead at admin@bluetext.in. All reports are acknowledged within 24 hours.
