Accessibility Statement

BlueTEXT aims to meet WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility expectations across navigation, content structure, and interactive controls.

Current Accessibility Controls

  • Semantic page structure with headings and landmarks
  • Keyboard-accessible menus, modals, and forms
  • Accessible labels on interactive controls
  • Responsive interface for desktop and mobile
  • Dark/light themes with persistent preference

Known Limitations

Some legacy pages may require incremental updates for complete consistency. These are reviewed continuously as content expands.

Feedback and Support

If you find an accessibility issue, contact us via Contact with page URL, issue description, and device/browser details.
