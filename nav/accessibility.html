Accessibility Statement Target standard: WCAG 2.2 AA Accessibility Features Keyboard-accessible navigation and interactive controls. Visible focus indicator for all focusable elements. Skip link for direct access to main content. Color and contrast-aware themes for light/dark modes. Reduced-motion support for users with motion sensitivity. Known Limitations Some third-party embedded content and external links may not fully follow this standard. We continue to monitor and improve these areas. Feedback and Support If you find an accessibility issue, contact us at codeon@bluetext.in with page URL and issue details.
