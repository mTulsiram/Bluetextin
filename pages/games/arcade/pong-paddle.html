Controls & Instructions

  • Up / Down Arrow Keys: Move your paddle up and down
  • W / S Keys: Alternative paddle movement control
  • Goal: Defeat the computer by bouncing the ball past their paddle! First to 10 points wins!

Save & Continue

You can save your current game scores, ball position, and speed to a file and load it back later to continue playing right where you left off!
