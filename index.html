Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data collected or tracked?

No. BlueTEXT.in is 100% client-side. We don't have servers, databases, or any data collection. Everything runs in your browser. Your data never leaves your device.

Does BlueTEXT.in work offline?

Yes! Our service worker caches all essential assets. Once loaded, BlueTEXT.in works completely offline. Perfect for planes, trains, and areas with no internet.

How do I use the tools?

Simply navigate to the category (Tools, Games, Education, etc.), select a tool or game, and it will open in your browser. No installation, no signup, no permissions needed.

What browsers are supported?

BlueTEXT.in works on all modern browsers: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, and Opera. We recommend the latest versions for best compatibility.

Can I contribute or report bugs?

Absolutely! BlueTEXT.in is open-source. Visit our GitHub repository to report issues, suggest features, or contribute code.

Cookie Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences.

BlueTEXT.in Cookie Usage

We use minimal cookies:

  • Session Cookies: Only when you're logged in
  • Preference Cookies: To remember theme settings
  • No Tracking: We never use cookies to track your behavior

Manage Cookies

You can manage cookie preferences in your browser settings. You can also clear cookies anytime without losing core functionality.

Third-Party Cookies
