## 📋 Platform Set Up Checklist (To Make Empty Pages Look Good)

### 1. Stripe Express & Payment Onboarding
- [ ] **Complete Onboarding:** Go to Stripe Express and complete identity verification (PAN and bank details). Payouts must be active.
- [ ] **Ko-fi Stripe Alert:** Log into Stripe via Ko-fi settings to clear the detected issue warning.

### 2. Buy Me a Coffee Profile Setup
- [ ] **Banner:** Change the default "CODE ON" header banner to your blue "BlueTEXT.in" branding.
- [ ] **Description:** Replace the default *"Hey, I just created a page here..."* text with:
  > "Hi, I'm Tulsiram Methre, AWS Solution Architect & Google Developer Expert. I build fast, client-side developer utilities at BlueTEXT.in. Supporting me keeps these tools 100% free and ad-free!"

### 3. Patreon & Stck.me Profiles
- [ ] **Patreon Page:** Click "Set it up now" to align your creator profile names, add a profile picture, and write a welcome post. Click **Publish Page**.
- [ ] **Stck.me Welcome Post:** Create at least one post introducing BlueTEXT.in and pin it to the top.
